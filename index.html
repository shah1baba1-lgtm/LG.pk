Know Your Rights

Fundamental Rights Guaranteed by the Constitution of Pakistan 1973

Constitution of Pakistan — Part II: Fundamental Rights

The Constitution of Pakistan 1973 guarantees fundamental rights to all citizens. These rights are enshrined in Part II (Articles 7 to 40) and are enforceable through the courts.

Categories of Fundamental Rights

Civil & Personal Rights

Article 9 — Right to Life and Liberty

No person shall be deprived of life or liberty save in accordance with law.

In Simple Words: Aap ki jaan aur azaadi qanoon ke haq mein hai. Koi bhi aapko baghair wajah ya qanoon ke khilaf nahi kar sakta.

Article 10 — Safeguards as to Arrest and Detention

No person who is arrested shall be detained in custody without being informed of the grounds for such arrest.

In Simple Words: Agar koi aapko arrest kare toh aapko batana zaroori hai ke kyun arrest kiya ja raha hai. Aapke paas apne pasand ke lawyer se milne ka haq hai.

Article 11 — Slavery and Forced Labour Prohibited

Slavery is non-existent and forbidden in all forms.

In Simple Words: Ghulami bilkul haraam hai. Koi bhi shakhs majboori ka kaam nahi kar sakta.

Article 12 — Protection against Retrospective Punishment

No law shall authorize the punishment of a person for an act or omission that was not punishable by law at the time.

In Simple Words: Jo kaam pehle qanoon ke mutabiq tha, uske baad naya qanoon ban jaye toh us purane kaam ke liye saza nahi di ja sakti.

Article 13 — Protection against Double Punishment

No person shall be punished for the same offence more than one time.

In Simple Words: Ek hi galti ke liye aapko dobara saza nahi di ja sakti.

Article 14 — Inviolability of Dignity

The dignity of man shall be inviolable. No person shall be subjected to torture.

In Simple Words: Har insaan ki izzat hamesha mehfooz hai. Kisi bhi shakhs ko torture ya zulm nahi kiya ja sakta.

Article 15 — Freedom of Movement

Every citizen shall have the right to remain in, and enter and move freely throughout Pakistan.

In Simple Words: Aap Pakistan ke kisi bhi hisse mein rehne, ghumna aur settle karne ke liye free hain.

Article 16 — Freedom of Assembly

Every citizen shall have the right to assemble peacefully and without arms.

In Simple Words: Aap aman se jama ho sakte hain, protest kar sakte hain. Lekin yeh sab peacefully hona chahiye.

Article 17 — Freedom of Association

Every citizen shall have the right to form or be a member of a political party.

In Simple Words: Aap kisi bhi political party mein join kar sakte hain ya apni party bana sakte hain.

Article 18 — Freedom of Trade, Business or Profession

Every citizen shall have the right to enter upon any lawful profession or occupation.

In Simple Words: Aap apni pasand ka koi bhi kaam, business ya profession choose kar sakte hain.

Political Rights

Article 19 — Freedom of Speech and Expression

Every citizen shall have the right to freedom of speech and expression.

In Simple Words: Aap apne khayalat zubaan ya likh kar express kar sakte hain.

Article 19A — Right to Information

Every citizen shall have the right to have access to information in all matters of public importance.

In Simple Words: Aapko public information milne ka haq hai.

Article 25 — Equality of Citizens

All citizens are equal before law and are entitled to equal protection of law.

In Simple Words: Qanoon ke saamne sab barabar hain. Kisi ko bhi discriminate nahi kiya ja sakta.

Article 25A — Right to Education

The State shall provide free and compulsory education to all children of the age of five to sixteen years.

In Simple Words: Har bachche ke liye 5 se 16 saal ki umar tak free education dena government zimmedari hai.

Economic & Social Rights

Article 23 — Right to Property

Every citizen shall have the right to acquire, hold and dispose of property.

In Simple Words: Aap Pakistan ke kisi bhi hisse mein zameen, ghar ya koi bhi property kharid sakte hain.

Article 24 — Protection of Property Rights

No person shall be compulsorily deprived of his property save in accordance with law.

In Simple Words: Koi bhi authority aapki property baghair qanoon ke nahi le sakti.

Article 37 — Promotion of Social Justice

The State shall promote, with special care, the educational and economic interests of backward classes or areas.

In Simple Words: Government ko backward areas aur classes ki education aur economy ko improve karni hai.

Article 38 — Promotion of Social and Economic Well-being

The State shall secure the well-being of the people by raising their standard of living.

In Simple Words: Government ko logon ki zindagi behtar banana hai.

Religious & Minority Rights

Article 20 — Freedom of Religion

Every citizen shall have the right to profess, practice and propagate his religion.

In Simple Words: Har shakhs apne mazhab ko follow karne, practice karne aur doosron ko batane ke liye free hai.

Article 21 — Safeguard against Taxation for Religious Purposes

No person shall be compelled to pay any special tax for any religion other than his own.

In Simple Words: Kisi bhi shakhs ko uske mazhab ke ilawa kisi aur mazhab ke liye tax dena nahi hoga.

Article 22 — Safeguards as to Educational Institutions

No person shall be required to receive religious instruction of a religion other than his own.

In Simple Words: Agar aap kisi school ya college mein hain, toh aapko apne mazhab ke ilawa kisi aur mazhab ki religious instruction nahi deni hogi.

Article 26 — Non-discrimination in Respect of Access to Public Places

There shall be no discrimination against any citizen on the ground only of race, religion, caste, sex, residence or place of birth.

In Simple Words: Public places par kisi ko bhi discriminate nahi kiya ja sakta.

Article 27 — Safeguard against Discrimination in Services

No citizen otherwise qualified for appointment in the service of Pakistan shall be discriminated against.

In Simple Words: Government jobs mein kisi ko bhi discriminate nahi kiya ja sakta.

Article 36 — Protection of Minorities

The State shall safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of minorities.

In Simple Words: Government ko minorities ki rights aur interests ka khayal rakhna hai.

Women & Children Rights

Article 25(3) — Special Provisions for Women and Children

Nothing in this Article shall prevent the State from making any special provision for the protection of women and children.

In Simple Words: Government aurat aur bachchon ke liye special laws bana sakti hai.

Article 34 — Full Participation of Women

Steps shall be taken to ensure full participation of women in all spheres of national life.

In Simple Words: Auraton ko har field mein hissa lene mein koi rukaawat nahi honi chahiye.

Article 35 — Protection of Family, Mother and Child

The State shall protect the family, the mother and the child.

In Simple Words: Government ko family, aurat aur bachche ki protection karni hai.

Article 37(e) — Just and Humane Conditions of Work

The State shall make provision for securing just and humane conditions of work.

In Simple Words: Workers ko fair treatment milna chahiye.

Legal & Judicial Rights

Article 4 — Right to be Dealt with in Accordance with Law

To enjoy the protection of law and to be treated in accordance with law is the inalienable right of every citizen.

In Simple Words: Har citizen ke liye qanoon ki protection milne ka haq hai.

Article 10A — Right to Fair Trial

For the determination of his civil rights and obligations or in any criminal charge against him a person shall be entitled to a fair trial.

In Simple Words: Agar kisi case mein aap par charge hai, toh aapko fair trial milne ka haq hai.

Article 14 — Inviolability of Dignity

The dignity of man shall be inviolable. No person shall be subjected to torture.

In Simple Words: Har insaan ki izzat hamesha mehfooz hai. Kisi bhi shakhs ko torture nahi kiya ja sakta.

Article 199 — Powers of High Courts

A High Court may, on the application of any aggrieved party, make orders directing any person to act in accordance with law.

In Simple Words: Agar koi government officer qanoon ke khilaf kaam kare, toh aap High Court mein complaint kar sakte hain.
